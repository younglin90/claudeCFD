# Air-water interface ringing: parameter-free fix campaign (2026-07-13/14)

Goal: make the full strict validation suite pass with a single scheme and zero
user-tuned coefficients. Status before the campaign: 12 of 13 core cases pass
tuning-free; the only failing criterion is the air-water 07-B acoustic guard
`p_smooth_local_tv_excess = 0.537 > 0.30` (N=400). Companion documents:
`research_D_free_conclusion.md`, `research_aw_ringing_cures.md`,
`diag_aw_packet.md`.

## What the artifact is (established by instrumented diagnosis)

A pressure-only wave packet (u and rho clean), wavelength 8-10 cells, born at
the interface cell (133) around step ~1080 when the pulse first touches the
3600:1 impedance jump, then filling the water-side smooth region. It survives
grid refinement (tv = 0.0 / 0.48 / 0.54 at N = 100/200/400). The final p is
the raw output of the implicit acoustic solve (never EOS-recovered in this
regime), so the packet originates inside `_solve_acoustic_ad`.

## Why the artifact resists local parameter-free mechanisms

1. Each step's reconstruction is TVD-legal: face values never leave the local
   cell envelope, so envelope- and jump-based selectors (C2, C2v2) cannot see
   the packet forming. It accumulates over ~1600 steps as a phase-coherent
   error, not as per-step overshoots.
2. It is temporally well resolved (omega*dt ~ 0.28): L-stable integrators
   (TR-BDF2) damp only the near-Nyquist band and correctly leave the packet
   alone; only first-order-in-time damping (BE) reaches it, and that damps the
   physical wave equally (amp 0.74).
3. It is spatially marginally resolved (4-5 points/wavelength): inside the
   physical band, so any scheme-level dissipation strong enough to remove it
   (minmod limiter: tv 0.26 but amp 0.63) also removes the transmitted wave.
4. Limiter sweep moves it strongly (superbee 0.54 / van Leer 0.40 / minmod
   0.26) while the kappa-order does not (C3): the driver is the compressive
   steepening of the limiter interacting with the impedance jump, not the
   linear dispersion order of the interior scheme.

Conclusion: the packet is generated by the interface treatment itself and sits
inside the physical band. Removing it without touching the physical wave
requires changing what the discretization does AT the interface, not adding
selective damping or raising interior order.

## Remaining avenues (each a substantial research task, all parameter-free)

1. **ACID face thermodynamics ported into the implicit acoustic solve**
   (Denner-Xiao-van Wachem 2018). Their pressure-based BDF2 solver passes this
   exact benchmark (0.3% error, no reported ringing). The differentiator is
   their acoustically-conservative construction of face density/enthalpy at
   the interface, not the time integrator (established here by T2b/BE data).
   A prototype exists in the frozen He2024 tree (`acid_interface`,
   explicit_mmacm_ex.py ~line 3874) and the five_eq_IMEX Newton family has a
   `face_thermo='acid'` option that never reached the imex_ad acoustic solve.
2. **Unified acoustic face state, full Phase A**
   (repo plan, flux_scheme_research_plan_2026_04_28.md:76-105): one (p*, u*)
   from the interface-band acoustic Riemann problem feeding BOTH the material
   advective fluxes and the pressure work. The single-face probe tested during
   diagnosis restored only the acoustic face velocity and changed nothing; the
   full unification (material mass/energy fluxes included) is untested.
3. **Exact two-material Riemann solver at detected interface faces**: solve
   the linear two-state acoustic Riemann problem with pure-phase impedances on
   both sides of the alpha jump (topology-detected, coefficient-free),
   bypassing the Wood-mixture cell state entirely in the face construction.

## Code state after the campaign

`solver/five_eq_IMEX/imex_ad.py` gained five gated, default-off options (all
verified bit-identical when disabled; 02_A golden value
2.764863893389702e-15 reproduced after every edit):
- `FIVE_EQ_IMEX_ACOUSTIC_SCHEME = theta | interface_be | trbdf2`
- `FIVE_EQ_IMEX_ACOUSTIC_RECON = component | characteristic | bvd | muscl3`
These are research instruments; the production default is unchanged.

## Bottom line

- The production method remains tuning-coefficient-free, and 12 of 13 core
  cases plus every regression gate pass under the strict verifier with one
  fixed configuration.
- The air-water TV guard (0.537 vs 0.30) is not fixable by any of the nine
  scheme-level parameter-free mechanisms tested here. The evidence points to
  the interface face construction as the necessary target; the three avenues
  above are the credible next steps, with the ACID port carrying the strongest
  external evidence.
- Until one of those lands, the honest paper position is unchanged: report the
  guard value as a limitation (already done in Sections 4.3, 4.7, and 5).
